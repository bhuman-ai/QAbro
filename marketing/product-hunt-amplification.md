# Product Hunt launch amplification

## Role

These are one-to-many launch posts that send an existing social audience to the
Product Hunt page after it is live. They amplify the launch; they are not
individual outreach, an upvote campaign, or a replacement for the compounding
search channel.

Replace `{PRODUCT_HUNT_URL}` only after the Product Hunt listing is live. Do not
replace it with the homepage URL, and do not ask people to upvote.

## Launch sequence

1. Publish the Product Hunt listing from the maker's personal account.
2. Copy the canonical Product Hunt launch URL.
3. Replace `{PRODUCT_HUNT_URL}` in both posts.
4. Publish the X post with the hero graphic.
5. Publish the LinkedIn post with the proof graphic.
6. Reply to real questions and feedback; do not send coordinated upvote
   requests or mass messages.

## Measurement

- Social exposure: X and LinkedIn post impressions.
- Social response: link clicks and substantive replies.
- Product Hunt exposure: launch-page views and Visit-button clicks when
  available.
- Website response: `product_hunt / referral / qa_mcp_launch`.
- Primary conversion: `first_qa_report_completed`.
- Interpretation floor: Do not judge social message response before the two
  posts produce 1,000 combined impressions. Do not judge the landing path
  before 100 attributed Product Hunt visitors.
- Continue if: The launch produces a completed first report or at least five
  percent of attributed visitors choose the install CTA while activation volume
  is still small.
- Change if: Social exposure produces no Product Hunt visits, or 100 attributed
  Product Hunt visitors produce no install clicks.

## Policy reference

Product Hunt encourages organic social sharing and authentic discussion. It
prohibits mass messages, incentives, and coordinated requests for upvotes:
https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/2690626-how-do-i-share-my-post
